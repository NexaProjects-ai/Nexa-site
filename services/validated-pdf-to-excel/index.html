FAQ

Before you send a file

Why not just use OCR or ChatGPT?

Those can extract text quickly. The paid part here is the checking layer: agreed schema, normalized datatypes, cross-row consistency, source traceability and a visible exception list instead of silent guesses.

What documents fit the fixed €129 scope?

Clear digital PDFs or clean scans with regular tables, up to 30 pages, one target schema and no handwriting or complex nested layouts.

Do you need confidential data?

No. Send only what is necessary for the task. For sensitive or regulated data, agree handling requirements before sharing files. The public sample uses fictional data only.

What if a value cannot be read reliably?

It is flagged for review. Ambiguity is not guessed or silently filled.

Can this become a recurring workflow?

Yes, but only after the manual proof is correct. Repeating supplier or reporting PDFs can then be assessed for a reusable extraction/validation pipeline.

Have a PDF table that needs to become usable data?

Send one representative sample and the columns you need. I’ll confirm whether it fits the fixed scope before any work starts.
