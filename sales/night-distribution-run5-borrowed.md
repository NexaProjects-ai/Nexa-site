## Channel comparison
BORROWED/VA route vs marketplace route:
- Buyer access: marketplace stronger immediately; VA route requires partner approval/outreach.
- Trust: VA partner can own end-client trust; Nexa only needs to prove reliable fulfillment to partner.
- Price pressure: expected lower on white-label overflow than commodity marketplace bidding, but unverified.
- Time to first cash: marketplace can be days if bid wins; partner route can also be days but requires first partner response.
- Repeatability: partner route potentially stronger because one relationship can feed multiple jobs.
- Controllability: direct partner list is reusable and not platform-algorithm dependent.

Verdict: GO as second motor test, not PRIMARY yet. Marketplace/live-demand route remains better evidenced; borrowed VA distribution is attractive only if one partner confirms recurring overflow.
